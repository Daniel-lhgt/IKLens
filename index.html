Common questions

Does IKLens generate the images?

No. IKLens gives you the prompt. You paste it into an AI image tool such as ChatGPT or Gemini, and that tool creates the image.

Which AI tools can I use the prompts with?

You can open ChatGPT, Gemini, Leonardo, Claude, Midjourney and Playground from IKLens. The prompt is text, so it also works in other tools that accept one.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign up to browse and copy prompts, save favorites, and submit prompts of your own.

Who owns the images I create?

You do. IKLens doesn't claim ownership of what you create. Check the terms of the AI tool you use as well.
